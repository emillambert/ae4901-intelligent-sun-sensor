# Curated research

These are **historical computational experiments and design references**, not deployable firmware. Use them to understand assumptions, reproduce selected studies, and choose physical tests. Source bundles and their original folder structures are preserved under `legacy/archives/`. See [`../docs/research-index.md`](../docs/research-index.md).

`geometry`: candidate orientations and controller failover. `optics`: mask CAD and comparative plot. `electronics`: SPICE reference netlist, not a validated KiCad schematic. `glints`: LDR-only ambiguity and temporal protection experiments. `bright_sun`: hypothetical direct-Sun/filter stress study.
